# My reel style

> Replace everything in *italics* with your own answers. Keep it short: a page is plenty.
> The colours, fonts and sizes go in `src/style/theme.ts`; this file is the "why".

## 1. Who it is for

- **Audience:** *e.g. kids 10–14 who like animals; or adults curious about science*
- **What they get from a video:** *e.g. one surprising fact they can tell a friend*
- **Language:** *e.g. English, simple words, no slang*

## 2. Voice and tone

- **Sounds like:** *e.g. a friend who just learned something amazing*
- **Never:** *e.g. shouting, clickbait that the video doesn't pay off, making fun of anyone*
- **Length:** *e.g. 20–40 seconds, 60–100 words*

## 3. Structure of every reel

1. **Hook** (first 2 seconds): *the most surprising sentence, said straight away*
2. **Body:** *2–4 short scenes, one idea each*
3. **Ending:** *a twist, a question, or "follow for more"*

## 4. Look

- **Feeling:** *e.g. clean and bright, like a notebook; or dark and dramatic*
- **Colours:** *main colour for attention, second colour for comparisons (set in theme.ts)*
- **Drawings:** *e.g. simple flat icons, no photos*
- **Text on screen:** *e.g. max 5 words at a time, big*
- **Series:** *e.g. "Animal facts #1, #2…" in the badge*

## 5. Rules

- *Every number and fact has a source, shown as a footnote.*
- *Only use music, images and fonts I'm allowed to use.*
- *No personal information (school, address, full name) in videos.*
- *Ask an adult before posting.* (if you're under 18)
