# Leave a short pause between lines when you record yourself.
# Source for the facts: Smithsonian Magazine, "Ten wild facts about octopuses".

[hook]
An octopus has three hearts.

[blood]
And its blood is blue, because it carries oxygen with copper instead of iron.

[hearts]
Two hearts pump blood through the gills.
The third one pumps it to the rest of the body.

[twist]
But when an octopus swims, that third heart stops beating.
That may be why octopuses would rather crawl.

[outro]
Follow for one weird animal fact a day.
