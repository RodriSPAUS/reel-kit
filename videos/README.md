# Your videos

Everything you make shows up here:

- `<name>.mp4`: the finished reel, ready to post
- `<name>-cover.png`: its cover picture
- `<name>-preview.mp4`: a quick lower-quality version to check before the final one
- `style-preview.png`: what your style looks like

On GitHub, open a file and press the download button to save it to your phone or computer.
